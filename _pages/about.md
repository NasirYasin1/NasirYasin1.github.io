I am a **Ph.D. researcher in Applied and Computational Mathematics** at **Old Dominion University (ODU)**, Norfolk, Virginia. My research interests include **scientific computing, numerical analysis, high-performance computing, and machine learning**, with a focus on developing efficient, accurate, and scalable computational methods for complex mathematical and physical systems.

My technical expertise includes **Python, MATLAB, R, C++, Fortran, CUDA, and Linux-based scientific computing**. I have contributed to computational modeling and numerical simulation projects, including funded research initiatives.

I hold an **M.S. in Applied and Computational Mathematics** from ODU, an **M.S. in Mathematics** from COMSATS University Islamabad, and a **B.S. in Mathematics** from International Islamic University Islamabad.

Alongside my research, I serve as a **Graduate Assistant** at ODU, teaching undergraduate mathematics courses and providing academic support through the Applied Calculus Lab, SMART Room, and online tutoring.

I welcome opportunities for **academic collaboration, research discussions, and professional networking**.
